# Frequently asked questions

### How do I download maps?

Maps are located in the "Browse" tab, inside the main menu. Make sure you are logged in to view them.

### Where can I find skins?

Skins are shared in the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/browse?appid=3440100) or the [#skins](https://discord.com/channels/994666749570076764/1158141048321749072) channel of the [discord server](https://discord.gg/XKNqkHzsr7).
